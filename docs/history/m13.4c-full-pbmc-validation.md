## Biological interpretation and boundaries

Strong B-lineage groups receive 97/97 B predictions. The two strongly supported
monocyte groups together receive 270 myeloid predictions among 274 cells, with
one B, one T and the two off-crosswalk predictions retained visibly. All 405 cells
in the three primary T groups receive T predictions. These are useful broad-level
supervised concordance observations, without proving subtype identity or truth.

Sparse primary evidence is not erased: group 3 has 21 myeloid and three B
predictions, exposing heterogeneous evidence worth inspecting. Group 5 has 106
myeloid predictions, adding lineage corroboration but not validating its M0
macrophage specificity. Group 8 illustrates added information beyond the retained
CellMarker run: its explicit database mapping had no positive T intersections,
yet NT predicts T for all 122 cells. Neither source replaces the primary label.

Both primary NK groups receive T predictions. Because NT cannot express NK,
these distributions are not an NK accuracy test or automatic disagreement. The
previously mixed T/NK group is not resolved by this model: NT yields no within-group
label heterogeneity there, and its vocabulary cannot adjudicate NK versus T.
No NK class, NK→T equivalence, manual correction or voting rule is introduced.

Unresolved primary cells receive 27 T and two myeloid predictions as separate
model evidence. All 29 primary annotations remain null; there is no rescue.

The three streams retain different roles: MAESTRO/marker-signature science assigns
the primary label; CellMarker supplies literature/database associations; EpiAgent
supplies an independently computed supervised scATAC prediction. They share the
underlying biological sample, may share prior biological knowledge, and are not
independent statistical trials. Training-set overlap with this sample has not
been excluded, and exact historical count provenance remains unproven. There is
no trusted held-out ground truth, calibrated confidence, ensemble score or vote.

## Checks, acceptance answers and next step

Exact pre/post hashes establish canonical H5AD/manifest, primary annotations,
marker evidence, historical candidate evidence, CellMarker output, checkpoint,
EpiAgent source/resources and frozen mapping invariance. All native class indices
and row positions are checked; all group counts reconcile to 1,119. An independent
pandas cross-tabulation reproduces every original-label group distribution, and
independent comparable-cell sums reproduce 899/904. Repeating only the comparison
calculation produces identical results; model inference runs once. No new
production component is needed for this scientific acceptance, so no broad test
suite or full Agent regression is run.

The acceptance questions are answered: full native inference and exact input/order
preservation succeed; primary labels are unchanged; native distributions and the
frozen mapping are given above; comparability and concordance have explicit
non-accuracy denominators; strong, sparse, NK-limited and unresolved cases expose
interpretable evidence without reassignment. EpiAgent is useful as an optional
validator under this qualified human/hg38 NT profile. The evidence combination is
sufficient to proceed to transparent annotation-quality integration within the
accepted scope. Another validator/benchmark is not a prerequisite for that
integration. Broader tissue/species claims, subtype accuracy, NK adjudication or
calibrated reliability would require separately scoped evidence.

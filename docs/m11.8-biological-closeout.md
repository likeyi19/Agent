## Biological findings

### Retained population and background

There are 263,747 observed barcodes and 1,119 selected candidates (0.4243%).
That low percentage is not catastrophic retention: rejected barcodes have median
QC depth 2 and 95th percentile 20. Selected candidates contain 16,672,714 of
20,276,156 canonical records (82.2282%). The frozen selection therefore retains
most record signal while removing a very large low-depth background.

The matching [10x Cell Ranger ATAC 1.2.0 dataset page](https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets/1-k-peripheral-blood-mononuclear-cells-pbm-cs-from-a-healthy-donor-next-gem-v-1-1-1-1-standard-1-2-0)
describes approximately 1,000 expected nuclei and 993 detected nuclei. Agent's
1,119 candidates are 12.69% above that vendor count, a plausible order of magnitude
under a different selection policy. This is a descriptive reference expectation,
not ground truth, cell-ID agreement, precision/recall, or a claim that extra
candidates are true cells. The older 1.1.0 page reports 1,195; it is not used as
the matching release comparator. Both the reference and interpretation were
reviewed on 2026-09-14; no vendor output matrix/barcode list was downloaded.

All selected TSS and nucleosome ratios are defined. Their depth and flank evidence

conditioned by selection and is not independent proof of cell identity.
Among all observed barcodes, 224,899 have undefined TSS enrichment and 70,415 have
undefined nucleosome signal. Defined rejected TSS values reach 469.307 amid very
low flank evidence; reporting only that maximum would misrepresent population
quality. The deepest rejected barcode has QC depth 694,350 and TSS 2.170, showing
that high depth alone did not define the retained population.

The frozen whole-artifact QC-contig histogram contains 20,195,815 records:
52.8710% at lengths 1–146, 33.0022% at 147–293, and 14.1268% at >=294;
3,903 are in the >=1001 overflow bin. These are the existing Agent bins, not
new metrics. The substantial short-fragment component and nucleosomal-length
component are consistent with interpretable ATAC signal. No fitted periodicity
or selected-only length histogram was inferred. General interpretation follows
[10x's web-summary description](https://www.10xgenomics.com/support/cn/software/cell-ranger-atac/latest/analysis/outputs/web-summary):
fragment-size profiles inform nucleosome organization, while TSS enrichment
depends on reference annotation and method. Agent's fixed incidence ratio is not
numerically interchangeable with vendor TSS scores or cutoffs.

**Concern retained:** the maximum selected nucleosome ratio is 9.132
(484 mononucleosomal / 53 nucleosome-free records), at QC depth 1,121 and TSS
11.285. The next-highest ratios are also elevated. The maximum selected depth is
153,425. These tails warrant caution for later analysis, but do not show population
collapse. The frozen policy deliberately has no maximum-depth or nucleosome gate;
this closeout does not silently add one or diagnose doublets. Every candidate is
not thereby certified as high quality. Cell calling remains `none/not_assessed`.

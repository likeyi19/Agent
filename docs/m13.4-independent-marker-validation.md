# M13.4 independent marker evidence: bounded qualification and limitation

**M13.4 NOT YET QUALIFIED.** A reproducible CellMarker resource and a private,
tissue-general association-evidence component now exist. The real-data check
establishes useful corroborating and competing associations, but does not establish
a scientifically justified identity-contradiction rule. Positive database
membership cannot establish marker exclusivity. This is an explicit scientific
qualification limit, not a request to tune thresholds against PBMC.

Baseline: branch `main`, HEAD `155b01607fbbfd9bda1bdb7efaee51d61cf02445`
(`Complete M13.3 generic marker annotation contract`), clean working tree. M13.3
was committed. Its source, RP, marker statistics and scoring remain unchanged.
Only `marker_validation.py`, its focused tests, and this document are added.
No public registration, Planner/compiler, Application, reports, authority/recovery,
model preprocessing or final annotated H5AD changes are included.

## Provisional component and identity contract

`validate_marker_annotation_candidates` takes exact primary result, RP gene-list,
and retained differential-marker paths/hashes; an explicit `KnowledgeResource`;
a pinned cell-type/context mapping; species; context; and explicit
`external-marker-associations.v1` profile. It returns
`agent.external-marker-validation.v1`, a private evidence object. These identifiers
describe the implemented provisional association profile, not an accepted general
biological validator or public artifact contract.

Inputs are the retained M13.3 `equivalent-candidates.json` and corresponding
M13.2 `genes.json` and `markers-signature-input.tsv`. The result binds all three
exact identities, plus raw database, exported table, source/release/retrieval,
normalization, mapping and context. Resource updates change result identity.
It does not rerun or feed back into primary science. Callers supply accepted
primary evidence; hash checks do not create scientific ownership authority.

Normalization uses only the database's explicit marker-to-Symbol association,
then uppercase for comparison with M13 symbols. Original marker, normalized
symbol and mapping status remain recorded. Empty/malformed symbols stay
unmappable; no original-marker fallback, Ensembl version stripping, synonym
search or HGNC guessing occurs. Multiple distinct usable symbols for the same
species/marker are ambiguous and excluded. Case-colliding primary genes fail.
An initial audit counted 23 mixed missing/present Symbol keys; inspection showed
these were missing annotations, not multiple nonempty mappings. Missing rows do
not invalidate other rows with an explicit usable symbol. Native GeneID/UniProt
values remain provenance, not a second inferred identifier authority.

Species, tissue vocabulary and disease context are explicit. PBMC qualification
uses Human, declared adult PBMC, tissue_type exactly Blood or Peripheral blood,
and cancer_type Normal. Umbilical cord blood and other fluids are excluded by the
inspectable mapping. This is a conservative broad tissue selection; it does not
certify that every retained source study is a healthy adult PBMC experiment.
Unsupported tissues/species or mismatched dependency hashes fail.

The qualification mapping explicitly maps naive/memory B candidates to B cell,
Monocytes to Monocyte, ActNK to Natural killer cell, CD4 T candidates to T cell,
and MacrophagesM0 to Macrophage. All except Monocytes use broader external types;
fine subtype, activation, memory and polarization are unvalidated. Alternative
broad B/T/NK/myeloid phenotypes are listed separately as incompatible *identity*
relationships. This does not declare their markers exclusive. Monocyte versus
macrophage is deliberately not an incompatibility assertion. Mapping is separate
from the algorithm; there are no group-number or immune-vocabulary branches.
Empty mappings produce `unmapped`; narrower-only evidence cannot establish a
broader candidate. No LLM alignment or general ontology machinery is used.

## Evidence interpretation and the unresolved scientific boundary

For each mapped type, the result records unique external marker-set size,
positive retained marker intersections, lower-RP markers, unmeasured markers,
measured genes absent from the retained marker table, mapping exclusions and
source row references. Source rows retain publication, assay and ontology fields
in the single pinned table. Repeated records do not multiply marker counts.
Per-group evidence is not duplicated per cell; canonical H5AD is untouched.

`validation_scope` is explicitly **mapped external type marker associations
only**, not validation of the full candidate identity. Presence of a compatible
positive association without competing associations yields `supported` within
that limited scope. This is a statement of observed overlap, not an acceptance
threshold, sensitivity/specificity estimate or calibrated probability. Competing
positive associations yield `insufficient_evidence` with an explicit review reason;
no overlap yields insufficient evidence. Unresolved primary candidates remain
unchanged with validation `not_applicable`. Accepted identity is always null.

The component cannot honestly produce a proven `conflicting` identity state from
this positive-only database. It records `conflict_assessment` as not established
by positive marker membership. Shared or cross-listed genes do not prove a
candidate wrong. Negative RP fold change also does not prove absence of RNA or
protein expression and is never used as a contradiction rule. This missing
justified contradiction mechanism is why full M13.4 qualification is withheld.

This knowledge dependency is separate from primary CIBERSORT signature scoring,
but shares measured genes and may share literature. It is not statistically
independent validation, ground truth, or an independent assay. The present
qualification cannot quantify that literature overlap or annotation accuracy.

Strong B groups include CD79A/CD79B/CD22/MS4A1 associations. Yet the same database
also lists some of these genes under other broad lineages. Group 7 retains T/NK
competition, but unweighted cross-type overlap also flags strong groups. Thus a
naive conflict rule would be unspecific; a threshold adjusted to rescue selected
PBMC groups would be unjustified tuning. Final conservative states are 11
`insufficient_evidence` and one `not_applicable`, while compatible and competing
association lists remain fully visible. This is useful evidence retrieval, not a
successful calibrated or categorical identity validator.

## Acceptance questions and next step

1. A reproducible tissue-aware resource exists for association evidence; full
   identity-validation suitability is not established.
2. The snapshot, export and provenance can be pinned; redistribution terms remain
   a separate unresolved deployment issue.
3. Comparison is deterministic without alias guessing, with honest exclusions.
4. Species/tissue context is explicit and fail-closed.
5. A small explicit broad alignment works; subtype equivalence remains unproven.
6. Support associations and insufficient evidence are honest; a scientifically
   justified definitive conflict state remains missing.
7. Primary scoring and external knowledge remain separate and immutable.
8. Real retained data show corroborating and competing associations, but also
   demonstrate why raw overlap is insufficient for categorical validation.
9. The component is tissue-general; only this human PBMC example was exercised.
10. No automatic candidate-to-accepted-identity policy is justified.

Next, qualify a small source-cited discriminating-evidence contract that can
establish contradiction or independent support at a declared identity granularity
and assay scope. It may require independently curated atlas/supervised evidence;
do not manufacture specificity from CellMarker set membership or tune a marker
count threshold. Keep this separate from primary annotation and public integration.
Focused validation tests (23) plus the nearby M13.3 regression (19) passed:
42 passed in 1.15 seconds. The full XLSX-to-TSV export was independently
byte-reproduced. Retained M13.2 genes, filtered markers, RP, candidate evidence
and report match their original retention hashes; all four M13.3 accepted
source/test/document hashes remain unchanged. No full regression, benchmark,
second tissue, commit or push was performed.
